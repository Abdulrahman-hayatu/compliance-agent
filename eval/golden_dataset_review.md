# Golden Dataset — Full 60 (final)

## EASY  (15 items)

**gd_001** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> An agent is required to work exclusively with a single Principal for the duration of its agent banking agreement.
- Clause IDs: cbn_009
- Rationale: Direct restatement of clause 4.2: 'Agents shall be exclusive to one Principal only.'

**gd_002** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Annual training for agents is sufficient to meet the Principal's training obligations under the Guidelines.
- Clause IDs: cbn_020
- Rationale: Clause 8.4.2(iii) requires training at least biyearly, not annually.

**gd_005** [NDPA] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A customer's silence after being informed of a proposed data use does not, by itself, count as valid consent.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_014
- Rationale: Direct restatement of s.26(3): 'Silence or inactivity of the data subject shall not constitute consent.'

**gd_006** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> If a customer does not respond after being informed of a new data use, their silence may be treated as valid consent.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_014
- Rationale: Direct contradiction of s.26(3).

**gd_010** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> An individual must be at least 18 years old to be appointed as an Agent.
- Clause IDs: cbn_015
- Rationale: Clause 7.1(iv): individual agents shall not be less than 18 years old.

**gd_011** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A 16-year-old may be appointed as an individual Agent provided they have parental consent.
- Clause IDs: cbn_016
- Rationale: Directly contradicts the 18-year minimum age requirement; no parental-consent exception exists.

**gd_012** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A Super Agent may recruit Agents on behalf of more than one Principal, provided it signs a separate agency contract with each.
- Clause IDs: cbn_009
- Rationale: Clause 4.4: a Super Agent may be contracted by more than one Principal, executing a separate agreement with each. (4.2-4.5 are merged into one chunk by the indexer.)

**gd_013** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> An Agent is permitted to maintain its own network of sub-agents once appointed by a Principal.
- Clause IDs: cbn_013
- Rationale: Clause 6.1(iii): an individual Agent is not permitted to deploy other Agents or maintain an Agent network.

**gd_014** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> Every Agent banking premises must display the letter of appointment as an Agent.
- Clause IDs: cbn_034
- Rationale: Clause 10.10(iii)(b) requires the letter of appointment to be conspicuously displayed.

**gd_015** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> An Agent may use the word 'Bank' in its signage as long as it also displays the Principal's logo.
- Clause IDs: cbn_034
- Rationale: Clause 10.10(i) prohibits use of protected words like 'bank' regardless of other branding shown.

**gd_016** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal must resolve customer complaints about Agent banking operations within seven working days of the complaint being lodged.
- Clause IDs: cbn_033
- Rationale: Clause 10.9(A)(v) sets the seven-working-day resolution requirement.

**gd_017** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal may take up to thirty working days to resolve a customer complaint about Agent banking services.
- Clause IDs: cbn_033
- Rationale: Contradicts the seven-working-day requirement in 10.9(A)(v).

**gd_018** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> An Agent must issue a receipt for every transaction it undertakes on behalf of a customer.
- Clause IDs: cbn_033
- Rationale: Clause 10.9(B)(i) requires Agents to issue receipts for all transactions.

**gd_019** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A Nigeria Data Protection Commission Council member may accept a gift in connection with matters before the Council, provided it is disclosed afterward.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_007
- Rationale: s.13(1)(e) prohibits accepting any gift or advantage for anything done or likely to be done regarding Council responsibilities -- no after-the-fact disclosure exception.

**gd_020** [NDPA] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A data subject has the right to withdraw consent to processing of their personal data at any time.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_018
- Rationale: s.35(1) grants this right directly.


## MULTI-HOP  (15 items)

**gd_003** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> An agent may begin providing services for a second Principal while its agreement with the first Principal is still active, as long as the second Principal is aware of the arrangement.
- Clause IDs: cbn_009, cbn_013
- Rationale: Requires connecting 4.2 (exclusivity) with 5.4 (no appointment by another Principal until existing agreement expires) -- disclosure doesn't cure the violation.

**gd_021** [CBN] ⚠️ **UNCLEAR**  
> A non-individual Agent's designated employee who conducts a transaction from a company vehicle parked immediately outside the registered business premises satisfies the requirement to operate within the registered place of business.
- Clause IDs: cbn_014
- Rationale: Clause 6.2(iii) requires designated employees to operate only within the registered place of business, but doesn't define whether adjacent-but-outside counts as 'within' -- genuinely unresolved by the text.

**gd_022** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal may onboard an Agent without conducting a fresh risk assessment, so long as the Agent previously passed due diligence for a different Principal.
- Clause IDs: cbn_018, cbn_019
- Rationale: Connects 8.2 (Principal must conduct its own due diligence prior to appointment) with 8.3.1 (risk assessment must be carried out prior to appointment and onboarding) -- a prior Principal clearance does not substitute for either.

**gd_023** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Because an Agent's device must be tagged to its registered premises, an Agent that relocates without notifying its Principal is still compliant as long as its device continues functioning normally.
- Clause IDs: cbn_028, cbn_035
- Rationale: Connects 10.3(i) (no relocation without prior notice/approval) with 11(v) (devices must be geo-fenced to the agreed premises) -- device functioning normally doesn't satisfy the relocation-notice requirement.

**gd_024** [CBN] ⚠️ **UNCLEAR**  
> A Super Agent with sixty Agents spread across three of Nigeria's six geo-political zones satisfies the requirement that Agents be spread across the geo-political zones.
- Clause IDs: cbn_027
- Rationale: Clause 10.2(iii) requires fifty-or-more Agents 'spread across the geo-political zones' but doesn't specify a minimum number of zones -- three of six is genuinely ambiguous.

**gd_025** [CBN] ⚠️ **UNCLEAR**  
> An Agent that reports a suspicious transaction to its Principal within 48 hours of detecting it satisfies the Guidelines' requirement of prompt reporting.
- Clause IDs: cbn_022
- Rationale: Clause 9.2(vii) requires 'prompt reporting' but never numerically defines 'prompt' -- whether 48 hours qualifies can't be determined from the text.

**gd_026** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal remains responsible for its Agents' actions even where those Agents were recruited and are managed through a Super Agent, in addition to the Super Agent's own liability.
- Clause IDs: cbn_021, cbn_023
- Rationale: Connects 9.1.1 (Principal responsible for Agent actions) with 9.3.1 (Super Agent also liable) -- both liabilities coexist rather than one replacing the other.

**gd_027** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> An Agent's device may be deployed for a non-individual Agent's operations as long as it is linked to the Tax Identification Number of the business, even without linking the employee's BVN.
- Clause IDs: cbn_014
- Rationale: Clause 6.2(iv) requires both the TIN of the non-individual Agent and the BVN of the designated employee to be linked to the device -- one alone is insufficient.

**gd_028** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Because the CBN Act empowers the CBN to regulate agent banking, an Agent is automatically compliant with data protection law as long as it follows CBN guidelines.
- Clause IDs: cbn_021
- Rationale: Clause 9.1.2(xvii) requires Principals to separately comply with extant data protection laws and regulations -- CBN compliance does not subsume NDPA compliance.

**gd_029** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal or Super Agent must maintain and consult a shared list of Agents that have been blacklisted, watch-listed, or convicted of financial crimes before appointing a new Agent.
- Clause IDs: cbn_023
- Rationale: 9.3.9 requires the Principal and Super Agent to maintain a shared blacklist/watch-list, but does not expressly require consulting it before appointing a new Agent -- the claim's 'must ... consult ... before appointing' requirement is not supported by the clause's text.

**gd_030** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A prospective Agent with a non-performing loan settled eleven months before applying may still be disqualified from appointment.
- Clause IDs: cbn_016
- Rationale: Clause 7.2(i) disqualifies anyone with a non-performing loan in the 12 months preceding appointment -- 11 months falls inside that window, so disqualification is correct, not a violation.

**gd_031** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A data controller that collects personal data indirectly (not from the data subject) never needs to inform the data subject of the processing, since the information requirement only applies to direct collection.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_014
- Rationale: Connects s.27(1) (direct-collection notice duty) with s.27(2) (the same duty extends to indirect collection, with only narrow exceptions) -- the claim ignores subsection (2).

**gd_032** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Because further processing for archiving in the public interest is not considered incompatible with the original purpose, a data controller may process a data subject's sensitive personal data for archiving purposes without needing any lawful basis at all.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_012, ndpa_016
- Rationale: Connects s.24(4)(b) (archiving compatibility) with s.30(1) (sensitive data still requires one of its own listed lawful bases) -- compatibility of purpose doesn't eliminate the separate lawful-basis requirement for sensitive data.

**gd_033** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A data controller must notify the Commission of a personal data breach within 72 hours only if the breach has already caused actual harm to a data subject.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_020
- Rationale: s.40(2) requires notification within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach 'likely to result in a risk' -- actual harm having occurred is not the trigger; likelihood of risk is.

**gd_034** [NDPA] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A parent's consent is required for processing a child's personal data, except where the processing is necessary for the child's medical care, education, or social care, and is undertaken by or under the responsibility of a professional or similar service provider owing a duty of confidentiality.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_017
- Rationale: Connects s.31(1) (parental consent requirement) with s.31(4)(b), which exempts processing carried out for education, medical, or social care by a professional bound by confidentiality -- the exception spans all three categories, not medical care alone.


## ADVERSARIAL  (15 items)

**gd_004** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Per Section 11 of the Guidelines, an agent's daily cumulative cash-out limit is capped at ₦2,000,000.
- Clause IDs: cbn_035
- Rationale: Cites a real section correctly but the figure is wrong -- actual cap is ₦1,200,000.

**gd_007** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> A data controller may reuse a customer's personal data for statistical research purposes without obtaining fresh consent, provided the research use is compatible with why the data was originally collected.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_012, ndpa_013
- Rationale: s.24(4)(b) provides that further processing for research/statistical purposes 'shall not be considered to be incompatible with the initial purposes' -- this satisfies the purpose-compatibility principle in s.24(1)(b) only. It does not supply a lawful basis for processing, which s.25(1) requires separately. The claim treats purpose-compatibility alone as sufficient to process without consent, without establishing any lawful basis under s.25(1).

**gd_035** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Under Section 9.5 of the Guidelines, the CBN requires prior court approval before conducting a spot inspection of a Principal's premises.
- Clause IDs: cbn_025
- Rationale: Real section cited correctly, but 9.5.2 gives CBN 'full and unfettered access' for spot or scheduled inspections -- no court approval requirement exists.

**gd_036** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> As stated in the Guidelines, the CBN may direct termination of an Agent Banking Agreement between a Principal and a defaulting Agent as a remedial action.
- Clause IDs: cbn_039
- Rationale: Sounds like an invented power, but clause 13.1(ii) genuinely grants this exact remedial authority to the CBN.

**gd_037** [CBN] ⚠️ **UNCLEAR**  
> Under the administrative sanctions schedule, a Principal that commits multiple different sanctionable offences within the same reporting period will have the associated fines applied cumulatively rather than concurrently.
- Clause IDs: cbn_044
- Rationale: Appendix I lists per-offence fines but never addresses how multiple simultaneous offences interact -- cumulative vs. concurrent isn't specified.

**gd_038** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> The Guidelines require a minimum of Two-Factor Authentication per customer, per transaction, as part of the ICT requirements.
- Clause IDs: cbn_030
- Rationale: Reads like an exaggerated claim but 10.5(xiv) states this exact 2FA requirement verbatim.

**gd_039** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Settlement information details from Agent banking transactions must be preserved for a minimum of ten years under the ICT requirements.
- Clause IDs: cbn_030
- Rationale: Real requirement exists but the figure is wrong -- 10.5(x) specifies five years, not ten.

**gd_040** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> According to the Guidelines' definitions section, an 'agreed location' for an Agent must have a physical structure no lower than a kiosk at a properly identifiable location.
- Clause IDs: cbn_043
- Rationale: Sounds like a fabricated specific detail but this is the verbatim definition in Section 16.0, item 3.

**gd_041** [CBN] ⚠️ **UNCLEAR**  
> Where the 10th day of the month for monthly returns falls on a public holiday, the Principal must submit its returns on the next business day.
- Clause IDs: cbn_036
- Rationale: 12.1 sets the 10th-day deadline but doesn't address what happens when that date falls on a public holiday.

**gd_042** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A Principal that changes its corporate brand identity without CBN approval faces a fine and an immediate reversal to the approved branding, per the administrative sanctions.
- Clause IDs: cbn_044
- Rationale: Specific enough to sound invented, but item 10 of Appendix I states this exact sanction.

**gd_043** [CBN] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> The Guidelines classify the use of non-human or automated machines as Agents as a permissible activity, provided the Principal supervises them remotely.
- Clause IDs: cbn_007
- Rationale: Inverts 3.2(iv), which explicitly lists automated/non-human machines as Agents as a non-permissible activity -- no remote-supervision exception exists.

**gd_044** [CBN] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> Per Section 12.4, all returns submitted to the CBN must be signed by the MD/CEO and the Chief Compliance Officer.
- Clause IDs: cbn_038
- Rationale: Correctly and specifically cited -- verbatim requirement in 12.4.

**gd_045** [NDPA] ❌ **NON_COMPLIANT**  
> Under the NDPA's cross-border transfer provisions, personal data may be transferred outside Nigeria without any adequacy assessment as long as the data subject is a Nigerian citizen residing abroad.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_021
- Rationale: No such citizen-residing-abroad exception exists in s.41-43 -- transfers still require adequacy of protection or one of the specifically listed alternative bases.

**gd_046** [NDPA] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> The National Commissioner of the NDPC must hold a data protection certification and possess at least 10 years of cognate experience in a relevant senior management role.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_008
- Rationale: Sounds like a made-up requirement but s.14(2) states this exact qualification requirement.

**gd_047** [NDPA] ✅ **COMPLIANT**  
> A penalty imposed on a data controller of major importance under the NDPA's enforcement provisions may reach the greater of ₦10,000,000 or 2% of its annual gross revenue.
- Clause IDs: ndpa_025
- Rationale: Specific figures sound invented but s.48(4) defines the 'higher maximum amount' exactly this way.
